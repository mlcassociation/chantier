# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone working on or with chantier — issues, reviews, chats, conferences — holds
to the same bar: be technically direct about code and considerate about people.
Critique belongs to artifacts, never to individuals.

## Be

- Direct without being unkind; disagree with reasons.
- Clear about uncertainty — "I don't know" beats confident guessing.
- Careful with beginners: the whole point of this project is readability.

## Not be

- Harassment, personal attacks, or sustained derailing.
- Identity-based discrimination — including but not limited to race, gender,
  sexuality, disability, nationality, or age.
- Publishing other people's private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to a maintainer privately (GitHub message to the org owner,
or via a maintainer listed in the contributors graph). Reports are handled discreetly;
retaliation for a good-faith report is itself a violation.

Maintainers may moderate: edit, close, or delete messages; temporarily or permanently
ban contributors whose behavior repeats after a warning; reject PRs on conduct grounds.
Any maintainer may take any of these actions for the good of the project.
